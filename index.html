Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about HALAL PESA applications.

What is the minimum loan amount?

The displayed minimum loan amount is KSh 5,000.

What is the maximum loan amount?

The displayed maximum loan amount is KSh 250,000.

How much is the loan interest?

The displayed interest is 7% of the original requested loan amount, charged once rather than monthly.

Is there a commitment fee?

A separate KSh 99 commitment fee is disclosed during the application process. The payment recipient, purpose and refund conditions must be provided before payment.

Is the KSh 99 included in the 7% interest?

No. The KSh 99 commitment fee is separate from the 7% one-time interest.

What is the repayment period?

The displayed repayment period is 3 months. The actual repayment schedule must be confirmed in the loan agreement.

Does applying guarantee approval?

No. Every application is subject to assessment. Payment of a commitment fee does not guarantee loan approval or disbursement.

Can I apply through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can contact HALAL PESA directly using the WhatsApp button.

Should I send my M-PESA PIN or OTP?

Never! HALAL PESA should never ask you to disclose your M-PESA PIN, OTP, password or confidential security information.
